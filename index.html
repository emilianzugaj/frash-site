How it works

A card payment costs about 25 cents in fees, so nobody can pay a fraction of a cent with a card. frash collects one top-up, and every visit after that is an entry in your balance.

  1. Top upAdd money with a card or BLIK. The amount goes to your frash balance.
  2. Allow a siteThe site shows its limits: the most it can take per page and per minute. Nothing is charged until you press Allow.
  3. Browse without adsEach page takes its real server cost from your balance. You see every charge on your Spending page.

The limits are enforced

A site cannot take more than the limits you allowed. The wallet refuses a charge over a limit. You can remove a site's permission at any time.

Spam pays for itself

Every request costs its sender. A normal visitor pays a fraction of a cent. A bot that sends millions of requests pays for each one, and the site can use that money for the extra server capacity.

Pricing

frash has no subscription. You pay only for the top-ups you choose to make.

  • Top-ups in USD, EUR, PLN or GBP, by card or BLIK, through Stripe.
  • The smallest top-up is 0.50 USD or the same value in another currency.
  • Your balance is kept in US dollars. A top-up in another currency is converted at the market rate of that day.
  • Sites set their own price per visit. Most pages cost between 0.001 and 0.1 cent.

Terms and policies

What you buy

A top-up adds prepaid credit to your frash balance. You spend the credit on websites that accept frash. The credit is not a bank deposit and earns no interest.

Refunds

You can withdraw from a top-up within 14 days and get the unused part of it back. After 14 days, you can ask for the unused balance back at any time. We refund to the card or account you paid with, within 10 working days. Credit already spent on a site is not refunded by frash.

Cancellation

There is no subscription to cancel. You can stop using frash at any time and ask for your unused balance back.

Disputes

If a site charged you for something you did not get, write to us. Show the charge from your Spending page. We check it with the site.

Privacy

frash keeps your balance, the sites you allowed and each charge. If you sign in with Google or e-mail, we keep that sign-in to find your balance again. Stripe processes your payment details; frash never sees your card number. We do not sell data or show ads.

Delivery

frash is a digital service. Your credit is on your balance right after Stripe confirms the payment. Nothing is shipped.

Contact

Questions, refunds and disputes: the contact e-mail is coming soon. We answer within 2 working days.
